How this dashboard updates

Gabby and Jorgie each keep a note on their iPhone, organized by month with Expenses, Fixed and Income sections. On the 1st of every month a Shortcut emails both notes; new lines are categorized, fixed and recurring bills are detected, Gabby's pesos are converted to US dollars at that day's official rate (TRM), and a written analysis is added. Pick "Full year" in the Month menu for the yearly summary.
